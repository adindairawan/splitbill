How Split Bill AI Works

📸 1. Scan Your Receipt

Upload a photo of your restaurant receipt. Our advanced receipt scanner reads the image in seconds, extracting every food item, price, and fee without manual typing.

👥 2. Add Your Friends

Type in the names of the people sharing the meal. Simply tap the checkboxes to assign who ate what. Shared an appetizer? Check multiple names and we'll divide the cost evenly.

🧮 3. Auto-Calculate

Stop stressing over math. The app automatically splits taxes (PPN), service charges, and discounts proportionally based on exactly how much each person ordered.

Why use an Bill Splitter?

Dividing a group dining bill can be awkward and complicated, especially when dealing with proportional taxes, service fees, or missing currency symbols. Split Bill acts as your smart, neutral calculator. Whether you are dealing with Rupiah (Rp), Dollars ($), or Ringgit (RM), our intelligent backend processes the receipt context, applies international cash register rounding, and gives everyone a perfect, Item breakdown of exactly what they owe.
